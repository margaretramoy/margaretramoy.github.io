Research Statement

“Of all the forms of inequality, injustice in health is the most shocking and the most inhuman because it often results in physical death.” — Rev. Dr. Martin Luther King Jr.

As King argues, inequality in health is among the most “inhuman” forms of injustice. Health disparities arise when factors outside a person's control, such as income and geographic location, limit their access to medical care. As a result, individuals in low-resource communities are more likely to delay or forgo treatment, allowing preventable conditions to progress. Accordingly, I plan to investigate the financial or geographic effects that most strongly prevent patients in underserved communities from receiving consistent care. Through this research, I will use that evidence to design community-based interventions that make care more affordable and accessible.
